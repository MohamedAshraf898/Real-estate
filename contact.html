FAQ

Frequently asked questions

When will the building be completed?

Handover is planned for Q3 2027. We publish construction updates with photos every month.

Can foreign buyers purchase a home?

Yes. Our team works with local notaries and lawyers who guide international buyers through every step.

How does the payment plan work?

You pay in five installments linked to construction milestones: 20% at reservation, 20% at foundations, 20% at structure, 30% at 80% completion and 10% at handover.

Is parking included?

Most homes include one space in the underground garage. Extra spaces and storage rooms can be purchased separately.

Can I choose my own finishes?

Yes — until the structure stage you can choose from three interior palettes and adjust selected layouts.
